The Booth Agent

How I get you across New York with the Metro MCP — you never leave the code.

You

You say where you are and where you want to be, in your own words, mid-project. No maps app, no new tab. Two places is all I need; one place, and I say null.

Me and the metro's tools

I know the subway well enough to guess the route. The metro's tools tell me what is true tonight: the nearest stations, the delays, whether the line still stops where I think, when the next train comes — one call at a time. When tonight disagrees with my guess, tonight wins.
